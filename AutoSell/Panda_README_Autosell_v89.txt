Panda End Game autosell rules - Harsh / EndGame v88, MidGame v89 (4 Nov 2026) - RaidBot 8.4

Three levels, two files each:
  Harsh    - top accounts (judged hardest)
  EndGame  - established accounts
  MidGame  - accounts without much S/A gear yet; sells about half of End Game or less (22-44 % on 4 test accounts):
             lower score bar at +12 (1.0 instead of 1.5); B sets (Perception, Immortal, Stun, Cursed, Righteous,
             Regeneration, Stonecleaver) judged like A tier; 5-star S/A pieces taken to +8 for a look; Rare S/A/B
             with SPD rolled kept; finished Epic+ pieces kept with SPD + one good roll (any 6-star set too), SPD 2x,
             or (S/A without SPD) two good rolls / a good 2x; Mythic in S/A/B sets with two good substats goes to +12

  *_Battles12.txt - battles + inbox: hopeful pieces to +8, the ones with a chance go on to +12 and are judged
  *_Storage.txt   - artifact / accessory storage clean-up

Silver per pass is capped by RaidBot's own max-upgrade setting.
